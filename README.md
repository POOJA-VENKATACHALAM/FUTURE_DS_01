# FUTURE_DS_01 – Superstore Sales & Profit Analysis

## 📊 Future Interns – Data Science & Analytics Task 1

This project is completed as part of **Future Interns Data Science & Analytics Internship – Task 1**.

The objective of this project is to analyze the **Superstore dataset** using Power BI and identify important sales, profit, product, category, and regional performance insights.

---

## 🎯 Project Objective

The dashboard is designed to answer the following business questions:

* Which products generate the most revenue?
* How do sales change over time?
* Which categories or regions are most profitable?
* Where should the business focus to grow faster?

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

* **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard development and visualization
* **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
* **DAX** – Measures and calculations
* **Microsoft Excel** – Dataset

---

## 📁 Dataset

The project uses the **Sample Superstore dataset**, containing information about:

* Orders
* Products
* Categories
* Sales
* Profit
* Quantity
* Regions
* States
* Order Dates
* Customers
* Sub-Categories

The data was prepared and transformed before creating the dashboard.

---

## 🧹 Data Preparation

The following data preparation steps were performed:

* Checked the dataset for missing values
* Checked for duplicate records
* Verified and corrected data types
* Converted date fields to appropriate date formats
* Reviewed the available columns and selected relevant fields
* Prepared the data for analysis in Power BI
* Created required calculations and measures using DAX

---

## 📈 Dashboard

The Power BI dashboard provides an interactive view of the Superstore business performance.

### Key Dashboard Components

* **Total Sales**
* **Total Profit**
* **Total Quantity**
* **Sales Trend Over Time**
* **Profit by Category**
* **Sales/Performance by Region**
* **Top Performing Products**
* **Interactive Filters and Slicers**

## 🔍 Key Insights

The analysis provides insights into:

1. The categories and products contributing significantly to overall sales and revenue.
2. Changes in sales performance over time.
3. Differences in profitability across product categories.
4. Regional differences in sales and profit performance.
5. High-performing products that contribute strongly to business revenue.
6. Areas where the business can focus its efforts to improve future growth.

---

## 💡 Business Recommendations

Based on the dashboard analysis:

* Focus marketing and sales efforts on high-performing product categories.
* Identify and promote products that generate strong revenue and profit.
* Analyze low-profit products and review their pricing or discount strategies.
* Focus expansion efforts on profitable regions.
* Monitor sales trends to identify periods of high and low demand.
* Use data-driven insights to improve future sales and profitability.
